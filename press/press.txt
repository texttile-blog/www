TEXTTILE · PRESS KIT · www.texttile.blog/press.html

The name
  Texttile meaning: an entry is text, and its gallery is tiles.

The line under the name
  The Multiplayer Blog Engine

The short form
  One entry. Many authors. No conflicts.

In fifty words
  Texttile is a blog engine for more than one writer. Everyone on
  the blog can be in the same entry at the same time, gallery of
  photos and videos included. It ships as one Docker container with
  SQLite inside, and needs no other service.

In three paragraphs
  Texttile is a blog engine built for writing together. Most
  engines give an entry one author and a lock. Texttile gives it
  company: everyone on the blog can open the same entry at the same
  time. One person has the text and types, the others see the words
  appear and can take the text over with one tap. The gallery is
  never locked, so tiles move under everyone's hands at once.

  An entry is text and tiles. The gallery holds photos and videos
  in a square grid, and readers get a lightbox. Save version keeps
  the title and the text as they stand, word by word. One password
  can guard the whole blog: a word you hand around, not a login.

  Texttile is open source, written in Elixir. It ships as one
  Docker container with SQLite inside, and needs no other service.
  The demo at www.texttile.blog starts a real Texttile. It runs for
  24 hours, then it stops.
